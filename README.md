# ISMAILIFY Premium E-commerce

Build a premium, high-end fashion e-commerce website for my clothing brand, ISMAILIFY.

Brand: ISMAILIFY

Tagline: “Style. Confidence. Identity.”

Use ONLY these brand colors:

- Deep Navy #0B1020

- Electric Violet #7C3AED

- White #FFFFFF

Create a luxury, modern, eye-catching fashion experience with bold typography, clean spacing, premium product photography, elegant animations, subtle gradients, and excellent visual hierarchy. Make it look like a professionally designed $10,000+ fashion website, NOT a generic template.

Create these pages:

1. Home

2. Shop

3. Men’s Shirts

4. Trousers

5. Shoes

6. Belts

7. Caps

8. Bags

9. Product Details

10. About

11. Contact

12. Cart / Checkout

Homepage should include:

- Premium hero section with “Elevate Your Everyday Style.”

- Shop Now CTA

- Featured collections

- Best sellers

- New arrivals

- Promotional banner

- Why choose ISMAILIFY

- Newsletter signup

- Premium footer

Products should include Men’s Shirts, Trousers, Shoes, Belts, Caps, and Bags, with professional product cards showing image, name, price, rating, wishlist, and Add to Cart.

Add product filtering, search, category navigation, product details, size/color selection where appropriate, cart functionality, quantity controls, and a clean checkout experience.

Make the website fully responsive on desktop, tablet, and mobile. Use subtle hover effects, smooth scrolling, tasteful animations, premium transitions, and optimized layouts.

IMPORTANT: Keep the design sophisticated and uncluttered. Use ISMAILIFY consistently throughout the website. Do not invent fake customer reviews or claim fake business results. Use high-quality fashion placeholder images that I can replace later.

Make the final result feel like a premium international fashion brand: bold, elegant, memorable, modern, and conversion-focused.
